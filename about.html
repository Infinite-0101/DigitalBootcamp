Our story

A foothold shouldn't require a head start.

Foothold exists because the entry-level jobs young people need most now ask for experience most young people haven't had the chance to get. We built a program to close that gap — paid, tuition-free, and built around what employers are actually hiring for.

The problem

Youth unemployment isn't a skills problem alone.

It's an access problem. Traditional bootcamps charge tuition young jobseekers don't have, or expect savings to cover months without income. Traditional job training rarely maps to what employers are hiring for today.

Our approach

So we removed the barriers, not the standards.

Foothold pays participants to train, charges no tuition, and builds every track's curriculum directly with hiring partners — so what you learn in week 3 is what you'll be asked to do in your first job.

What we hold to

The standards behind the program

Paid, not volunteered

Training full-time for 12 weeks is real work. We pay a stipend so it doesn't require savings, a second job, or family support to get through.

Built with employers

Every curriculum is reviewed each cohort by our hiring partners, so the skills taught match the roles actually open in the market.

Support doesn't end at graduation

Career coaching continues until you're placed — not for a fixed number of weeks, however long it takes.
