Four tracks, one format

Choose the track that matches the job you want.

Every track runs 12 weeks, full-time, in the same cohort structure — live instruction in the morning, project work in the afternoon, and a real client or capstone project in week 10 through 12.

Track 01 Web Development 12 weeks · full-time Cohorts of 18–22 Entry-level, no coding background needed

Web Development

You'll go from your first line of code to a deployed, working website — built the way small agencies and in-house teams actually work.

  • HTML5 & semantic markup
  • CSS layout, Flexbox & Grid
  • JavaScript fundamentals
  • Git & GitHub workflows
  • Responsive, mobile-first design
  • Working with a design handoff
  • Debugging & browser dev tools
  • Deploying a live site

Typical roles after graduation: Junior Web Developer, Front-End Support, Website Coordinator.

Track 02 Digital Marketing 12 weeks · full-time Cohorts of 18–22 Entry-level, no coding background needed

Digital Marketing

You'll plan, launch, and measure real campaigns — the exact skill set small and mid-size businesses hire for first.

  • SEO fundamentals
  • Paid social campaigns
  • Email marketing & automation
  • Content calendars & copywriting
  • Analytics & reporting dashboards
  • Brand voice & audience research
  • Budget basics & ad spend tracking
  • Client presentation skills

Typical roles after graduation: Marketing Coordinator, Social Media Assistant, SEO Associate.

Track 03 IT Support 12 weeks · full-time Cohorts of 18–22 Entry-level, no coding background needed

IT Support

You'll build the troubleshooting instincts and ticketing discipline that help desks and IT teams rely on every day.

  • Hardware & OS troubleshooting
  • Networking fundamentals
  • Help-desk ticketing systems
  • Account & access management
  • Customer communication under pressure
  • Basic cybersecurity hygiene
  • Windows & macOS support
  • Documentation & knowledge bases

Typical roles after graduation: Help Desk Technician, IT Support Associate, Desktop Support.

Track 04 UX & Product Design 12 weeks · full-time Cohorts of 18–22 Entry-level, no design background needed

UX & Product Design

You'll learn to turn a real problem into a tested, working prototype — and build the portfolio that gets junior design roles noticed.

  • User research & interviews
  • Wireframing & prototyping in Figma
  • Information architecture
  • Usability testing
  • Design systems basics
  • Accessibility fundamentals
  • Presenting & defending design decisions
  • Building a portfolio case study

Typical roles after graduation: Junior Product Designer, UX Research Assistant, Design Coordinator.
