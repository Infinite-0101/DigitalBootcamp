Common questions

Before you apply

Is the program really free?

Yes. Foothold is funded by workforce development grants and employer partners, so there's no tuition cost to you. You'll also receive a weekly stipend while you train.

Do I need any prior experience?

No. Every track is built for people starting from zero in that field. You just need to be comfortable with everyday computer use — email, web browsing, that kind of thing.

What if I have to work while I train?

The program is full-time, Monday through Friday, and the stipend is designed to make that possible. If part-time work is unavoidable, mention it on your matching call and we'll see what can be arranged.

What happens if I don't get hired right away?

Placement support doesn't stop at graduation. You'll keep working with a career coach — resume reviews, mock interviews, and introductions to hiring partners — until you land a role.

Can I apply if I'm outside the metro area?

The bootcamp currently runs in person, so you'll need to live in or be able to relocate to the program's metro area for the 12-week duration. Mention your situation on the application and we'll let you know what's possible.
